          Eating targets

          Fat and protein are per lb of your 7-day average weight. Carbs fill the calories left over. Everything updates as your weight changes.

          Set up your targets

          Your stats give a starting estimate of what you burn a day (TDEE), using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula most calculators use. It's a starting guess: after about 4 weeks of logging meals and weigh-ins, the Trends tab measures your real maintenance and offers to update it.

          Weight starts as your 7-day average. A deficit of 500 kcal a day is about 1 lb a week; 0 eats at maintenance.

          Log measurements

          In inches. Fill in what you measured and leave the rest blank. Measure in the morning, breathing out normally, with the tape snug but not pressing in. Waist goes around at your navel.
